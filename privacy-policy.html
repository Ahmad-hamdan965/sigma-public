Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Overview

SIGMA XPRESS ("we", "our", "us") provides international freight and logistics services between China and the United Arab Emirates, including a customer mobile application and a staff administration platform (together, the "Services"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account or using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and operate our Services:

  • Account information: full name, username, client code, phone number, email address, and shipping/billing address.
  • Shipment information: details of goods you send through us, including weight, volume, package count, goods category, and tracking status.
  • Billing information: invoices, payment status, and currency preferences related to your shipments. We do not collect or store credit card numbers within the app.
  • Communications: quote requests, support messages, and any other information you choose to send us.
  • Push notification tokens: if you enable notifications, we store a device token to send you shipment updates and offers.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as app version and device type, used only for troubleshooting.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your customer account.
  • To process, track, and deliver your shipments.
  • To generate invoices and manage payment status.
  • To respond to quote requests and customer support inquiries.
  • To send you shipment status updates, receipts, and — if you opt in — promotional offers.
  • To maintain the security and proper functioning of our Services.

Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Our own staff (sales, warehouse, accounting) strictly to fulfill your shipment and billing needs.
  • Shipping, customs, and logistics partners, only as necessary to move your goods between China and the UAE.
  • Service providers that host our infrastructure (e.g. cloud hosting and database providers) under confidentiality obligations.
  • Government or customs authorities, where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain your account and shipment information for as long as your account is active and as needed to comply with our legal, accounting, and reporting obligations. You may request deletion of your account as described below.

Your Rights and Choices

  • You can review and update your profile information by contacting our support team.
  • You can opt out of promotional push notifications at any time from your device settings.
  • You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • You can request that we delete your account and associated personal information, subject to any shipments or invoices we are legally required to keep records of.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

Our Services are intended for business use by adults arranging freight shipments. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access-controlled staff accounts — to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
